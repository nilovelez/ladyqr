LadyQR
======

LadyQR makes QR codes right in your browser: what you type never leaves it.
It also comes with an optional link shortener for your own domain.

https://github.com/nilovelez/ladyqr


Requirements
------------

- A web hosting with PHP 7.4 or later. No database is needed.
- For the link shortener, an Apache server that allows .htaccess files
  (most shared hostings do). The QR code generator works without them.
- HTTPS is recommended, so the admin's password travels encrypted.


Installing
----------

1. Unzip ladyqr-latest.zip on your computer. You get a folder called
   "ladyqr".

2. Upload what is INSIDE the "ladyqr" folder to your site, by FTP or with
   your hosting's file manager. It can go in the root of your domain or in a
   folder of it, such as /qr/.

3. Open your site in a browser. The installer starts by itself and asks:
   - the language of the installer,
   - which components you want to install: the QR code generator alone, or
     the generator with the link shortener,
   - and the settings for that choice.

   If you choose the link shortener, write down the user and the password it
   shows at the end. The password is not shown again.

Install it right after uploading it: until it is installed, every visit to
your site goes to the installer, and whoever gets there first could install
it.


Only the QR code generator, with nothing to attack
--------------------------------------------------

If you only want the QR code generator, you can also delete the "admin"
folder from your site instead of using the installer. Without it there is no
installer and no admin: your site is just the QR code generator.


Updating
--------

Unzip the new ladyqr-latest.zip and upload what is inside the "ladyqr"
folder over your current files, replacing them.

Your settings (ladyqr-config.php), your short links (ladyqr-data.php) and
your .htaccess are not in the zip, so they are kept.

If you had deleted the "admin" folder, delete it again after updating.


Installing again
----------------

Delete the ladyqr-config.php file from your site and open it in a browser:
the installer starts again. Your short links, in ladyqr-data.php, are kept.

-----------------------

See the "Troubleshooting" section of the README:
